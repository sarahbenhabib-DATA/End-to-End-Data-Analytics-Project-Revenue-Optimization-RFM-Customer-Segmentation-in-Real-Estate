Proyecto real de Data Analytics y Yield Management para el sector inmobiliario turístico en Benidorm. Limpieza de datos, detección de anomalías de precios, segmentación RFM y dashboards interactivos. Stack: Python, R y Power BI.
# Real Estate Yield Management & Revenue Analytics — Benidorm Portfolio

## 📌 Project Overview
Analysis of real operational data from a vacation rental agency in Benidorm to optimize Revenue/Yield Management, detect pricing anomalies, and perform customer RFM segmentation.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Python:** Data cleaning, manipulation, and anomaly detection algorithms
- **R (tidyverse):** Advanced statistical analysis and seasonal pattern identification
- **Power BI:** Interactive executive dashboards for revenue strategy

## 📊 Key Business Outcomes
- **Pricing Optimization:** Identified seasonality patterns to maximize occupancy and profit margin.
- **Leakage Prevention:** Detected critical pricing anomalies and hidden revenue losses.
- **Client Segmentation:** RFM analysis to target VIP clients and boost repeat bookings.
